Public Law 108-49
108th Congress

                                 An Act


 
To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 
  141 Erie Street in Linesville, Pennsylvania, as the ``James R. Merry 
          Post Office''. <<NOTE: July 14, 2003 -  [H.R. 981]>> 

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. DESIGNATION.

    The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 141 Erie 
Street in Linesville, Pennsylvania, shall be known and designated as the 
``James R. Merry Post Office''.

SEC. 2. REFERENCES.

    Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other 
record of the United States to the facility referred to in section 1 
shall be deemed to be a reference to the ``James R. Merry Post Office''.

    Approved July 14, 2003.
