Public Law 118-102
118th Congress

                                 An Act


 
To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 
 90 McCamly Street South in Battle Creek, Michigan, as the ``Sojourner 
        Truth Post Office''. <<NOTE: Oct. 1, 2024 -  [S. 3851]>> 

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. SOJOURNER TRUTH POST OFFICE.

    (a) Designation.--The facility of the United States Postal Service 
located at 90 McCamly Street South in Battle Creek, Michigan, shall be 
known and designated as the ``Sojourner Truth Post Office''.
    (b) References.--Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, 
paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to 
in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the ``Sojourner 
Truth Post Office''.

    Approved October 1, 2024.
