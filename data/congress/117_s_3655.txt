Public Law 117-222
117th Congress

                                 An Act


 
 To amend the Civil Rights Cold Case Records Collection Act of 2018 to 
extend the termination date of the Civil Rights Cold Case Records Review 
               Board. <<NOTE: Dec. 5, 2022 -  [S. 3655]>> 

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled, <<NOTE: Civil 
Rights Cold Case Investigations Support Act of 2022. 44 USC 101 note.>> 
SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Civil Rights Cold Case Investigations 
Support Act of 2022''.
SEC. 2. CIVIL RIGHTS COLD CASE RECORDS REVIEW BOARD EXTENSION OF 
                    TENURE.

    Section 5(n)(1) of Civil Rights Cold Case Records Collection Act of 
2018 (44 U.S.C. 2107 note; Public Law 115-426) is amended--
            (1) by striking ``4 years'' and inserting ``7 years''; and
            (2) by striking ``4-year period'' and inserting ``7-year 
        period''.

    Approved December 5, 2022.
