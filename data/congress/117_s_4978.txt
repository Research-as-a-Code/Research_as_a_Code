Public Law 117-356
117th Congress

                                 An Act


 
To amend the Public Health Service Act to reauthorize the State offices 
      of rural health program. <<NOTE: Jan. 5, 2023 -  [S. 4978]>> 

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled, <<NOTE: State Offices of 
Rural Health Program Reauthorization Act of 2022.>> 
SECTION 1. <<NOTE: 42 USC 201 note.>>  SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``State Offices of Rural Health Program 
Reauthorization Act of 2022''.
SEC. 2. STATE OFFICES OF RURAL HEALTH.

    Section 338J(i)(1) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 
254r(i)(1)) is amended by striking ``fiscal years 2018 through 2022'' 
and inserting ``fiscal years 2023 through 2027''.

    Approved January 5, 2023.
