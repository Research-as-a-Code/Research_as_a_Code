Public Law 117-270
117th Congress

                                 An Act


 
To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 
   275 Penn Avenue in Salem, Ohio, as the ``Howard Arthur Tibbs Post 
            Office''. <<NOTE: Dec. 27, 2022 -  [H.R. 2473]>> 

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. HOWARD ARTHUR TIBBS POST OFFICE.

    (a) Designation.--The facility of the United States Postal Service 
located at 275 Penn Avenue in Salem, Ohio, shall be known and designated 
as the ``Howard Arthur Tibbs Post Office''.
    (b) References.--Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, 
paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to 
in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the ``Howard 
Arthur Tibbs Post Office''.

    Approved December 27, 2022.
