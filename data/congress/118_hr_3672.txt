Public Law 118-12
118th Congress

                                 An Act


 
To designate the clinic of the Department of Veterans Affairs in Indian 
 River, Michigan, as the ``Pfc. Justin T. Paton Department of Veterans 
        Affairs Clinic''. <<NOTE: July 28, 2023 -  [H.R. 3672]>> 

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. DESIGNATION OF PFC. JUSTIN T. PATON DEPARTMENT OF 
                              VETERANS AFFAIRS CLINIC.

    (a) Designation.--The community-based outpatient clinic of the 
Department of Veterans Affairs located at 5739 Hwy M-68, Indian River, 
Michigan, shall after the date of the enactment of this Act be known and 
designated as the ``Pfc. Justin T. Paton Department of Veterans Affairs 
Clinic''.
    (b) Reference.--Any reference in any law, regulation, map, document, 
paper, or other record of the United States to the clinic referred to in 
subsection (a) shall be considered to be a reference to the ``Pfc. 
Justin T. Paton Department of Veterans Affairs Clinic''.

    Approved July 28, 2023.
