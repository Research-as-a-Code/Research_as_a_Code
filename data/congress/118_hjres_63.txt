Public Law 118-29
118th Congress

                            Joint Resolution


 
Providing for the appointment of Antoinette Bush as a citizen regent of 
 the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution. <<NOTE: Dec. 21, 
                        2023 -  [H.J. Res. 63]>> 

    Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United 
States of America in Congress assembled, That in accordance with section 
5581 of the Revised Statutes of the United States (20 U.S.C. 43) the 
vacancy on the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution, in the 
class other than Members of Congress, occurring by reason of the 
expiration of the term of Steve Case of Virginia on April 25, 2023, is 
filled by the appointment of Antoinette Bush of the District of 
Columbia. <<NOTE: Time period. Effective date.>>  The appointment is for 
a term of six years, beginning on the later of April 25, 2023, or the 
date of enactment of this joint resolution.

    Approved December 21, 2023.
