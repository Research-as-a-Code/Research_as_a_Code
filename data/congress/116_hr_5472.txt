Public Law 116-341
116th Congress

                                 An Act


 
 To redesignate the Jimmy Carter National Historic Site as the ``Jimmy 
   Carter National Historical Park''. <<NOTE: Jan. 13, 2021 -  [H.R. 
                                5472]>> 

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled, <<NOTE: Jimmy Carter 
National Historical Park Redesignation Act. 16 USC 410hhhh note.>> 
SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Jimmy Carter National Historical Park 
Redesignation Act''.
SEC. 2. DESIGNATION OF JIMMY CARTER NATIONAL HISTORICAL PARK.

    (a) <<NOTE: 16 USC 410hhhh note.>>  In General.--The Jimmy Carter 
National Historic Site shall be known and designated as the ``Jimmy 
Carter National Historical Park''.

    (b) Amendments to Public Law 100-206.--Public Law 100-206 (54 U.S.C. 
320101 note; 101 Stat. 1434) is amended--
            (1) <<NOTE: 16 USC 410hhhh.>>  in section 1(a), in the 
        matter preceding paragraph (1), by striking ``National Historic 
        Site'' and inserting ``National Historical Park'';
            (2) <<NOTE: 16 USC 410hhhh-2.>>  in section 3--
                    (A) in subsection (a), by striking ``provisions of 
                law generally applicable to national historic sites'' 
                and inserting ``provisions of law generally applicable 
                to units of the National Park System''; and
                    (B) in subsection (d), in the second sentence, by 
                striking ``National Historic Site'' and inserting 
                ``National Historical Park'';
            (3) <<NOTE: 16 USC 410hhhh-5.>>  in section 6(2), by 
        striking ``National Historic Site'' and inserting ``National 
        Historical Park'';
            (4) <<NOTE: 16 USC 410hhhh, 410hhhh-2--410hhhh-6. 16 USC 
        410hhhh, 410hhhh-2--410hhhh-6. 16 USC 410hhhh.>>  by striking 
        ``historic site'' each place it appears and inserting 
        ``historical park'';
            (5) by striking ``historic site'' each place it appears and 
        inserting ``historical park''; and
            (6) by striking ``Historic Site'' each place it appears and 
        inserting ``Historical Park''.

    (c) <<NOTE: 16 USC 410hhhh note.>>  References.--Any reference in 
any law, regulation, document, record, map, or other paper of the United 
States to the

[[Page 134 STAT. 5133]]

Jimmy Carter National Historic Site shall be considered to be a 
reference to the ``Jimmy Carter National Historical Park''.

    Approved January 13, 2021.
