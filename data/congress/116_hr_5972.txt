Public Law 116-306
116th Congress

                                 An Act


 
To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 
500 Delaware Avenue, Suite 1, in Wilmington, Delaware, as the ``Mary Ann 
    Shadd Cary Post Office''. <<NOTE: Jan. 5, 2021 -  [H.R. 5972]>> 

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled, <<NOTE: Mary Ann Shadd 
Cary Post Office Dedication Act.>> 
SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Mary Ann Shadd Cary Post Office 
Dedication Act''.
SEC. 2. MARY ANN SHADD CARY POST OFFICE.

    (a) Designation.--The facility of the United States Postal Service 
located at 500 Delaware Avenue, Suite 1, in Wilmington, Delaware, shall 
be known and designated as the ``Mary Ann Shadd Cary Post Office''.
    (b) References.--Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, 
paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to 
in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the ``Mary Ann 
Shadd Cary Post Office''.

    Approved January 5, 2021.
