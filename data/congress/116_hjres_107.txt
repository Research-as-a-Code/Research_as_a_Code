Public Law 116-225
116th Congress

                            Joint Resolution


 
 Making further continuing appropriations for fiscal year 2021, and for 
       other purposes. <<NOTE: Dec. 18, 2020 -  [H.J. Res. 107]>> 

    Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United 
States of America in Congress assembled, <<NOTE: Further Additional 
Continuing Appropriations Act, 2021.>>  That the Continuing 
Appropriations Act, 2021 (division A of Public Law 116-
159) <<NOTE: Ante, p. 711, 1041.>>  is further amended by striking the 
date specified in section 106(3) and inserting ``December 20, 2020''.

     This joint resolution may be cited as the ``Further Additional 
Continuing Appropriations Act, 2021''.

    Approved December 18, 2020.
