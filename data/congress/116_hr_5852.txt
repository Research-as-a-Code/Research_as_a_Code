Public Law 116-305
116th Congress

                                 An Act


 
  To redesignate the Weir Farm National Historic Site in the State of 
Connecticut as the ``Weir Farm National Historical Park''. <<NOTE: Jan. 
                        5, 2021 -  [H.R. 5852]>> 

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled, <<NOTE: Weir Farm 
National Historical Park Redesignation Act. 16 USC 410ffff note.>> 
SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Weir Farm National Historical Park 
Redesignation Act''.
SEC. 2. <<NOTE: 16 USC 410ffff note.>>  WEIR FARM NATIONAL 
                    HISTORICAL PARK.

    (a) In General.--The Weir Farm National Historic Site shall be known 
and designated as the ``Weir Farm National Historical Park''.
    (b) Amendments to the Weir Farm National Historic Site Establishment 
Act of 1990.--The Weir Farm National Historic Site Establishment Act of 
1990 (54 U.S.C. 320101 note; Public Law 101-485; 104 Stat. 1171; 108 
Stat. 4756; 112 Stat. 3296; 123 Stat. 1190) is amended--
            (1) <<NOTE: 16 USC 410ffff.>>  in section 2(2)--
                    (A) by striking ``historic site'' and inserting 
                ``historical park''; and
                    (B) by striking ``National Historic Site'' and 
                inserting ``National Historical Park'';
            (2) <<NOTE: 16 USC 410ffff-2.>>  in section 4--
                    (A) in the heading, by striking ``historic site'' 
                and inserting ``historical park'';
                    (B) in subsection (a), by striking ``Historic Site'' 
                and inserting ``Historical Park''; and
                    (C) by striking ``historic site'' each place it 
                appears and inserting ``historical park'';
            (3) <<NOTE: 16 USC 410ffff-3.>>  in section 5, by striking 
        ``historic site'' each place it appears and inserting 
        ``historical park''; and
            (4) <<NOTE: 16 USC 410ffff-4.>>  in section 6--
                    (A) in the heading, by striking ``historic site'' 
                and inserting ``historical park''; and
                    (B) by striking ``historic site'' each place it 
                appears and inserting ``historical park''.

    (c) <<NOTE: 16 USC 410ffff note.>>  References.--Any reference in 
any law, regulation, document, record, map, or other paper of the United 
States to the

[[Page 134 STAT. 4918]]

Weir Farm National Historic Site shall be considered to be a reference 
to the ``Weir Farm National Historical Park''.

    Approved January 5, 2021.
