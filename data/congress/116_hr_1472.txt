Public Law 116-328
116th Congress

                                 An Act


 
  To rename the Homestead National Monument of America near Beatrice, 
 Nebraska, as the Homestead National Historical Park. <<NOTE: Jan. 13, 
                         2021 -  [H.R. 1472]>> 

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. HOMESTEAD NATIONAL HISTORICAL PARK.

    (a) In General.--The first section of the Act of March 19, 1936 (16 
U.S.C. 450u), <<NOTE: 16 USC 410gggg.>>  is amended by striking 
``designated'' and all that follows through the end and inserting 
``designated the `Homestead National Historical Park'.''.

    (b) <<NOTE: 16 USC 410gggg note.>>  References.--Any reference in a 
law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United 
States to the unit of the National Park System known as ``The Homestead 
National Monument of America'' shall be considered to be a reference to 
the ``Homestead National Historical Park''.

    Approved January 13, 2021.
