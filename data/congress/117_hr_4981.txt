Public Law 117-51
117th Congress

                                 An Act


 
    To amend the Fentanyl Sanctions Act, to modify certain deadlines 
        relating to the Commission on Combating Synthetic Opioid 
          Trafficking. <<NOTE: Oct. 19, 2021 -  [H.R. 4981]>> 

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. MODIFICATION OF CERTAIN DEADLINES FOR COMMISSION ON 
                              COMBATING SYNTHETIC OPIOID 
                              TRAFFICKING.

    Section 7221(f)(2) of the Fentanyl Sanctions Act (133 Stat. 2273) is 
amended by striking ``270 days'' and inserting ``390 days''.

    Approved October 19, 2021.
