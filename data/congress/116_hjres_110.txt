Public Law 116-226
116th Congress

                            Joint Resolution


 
 Making further continuing appropriations for fiscal year 2021, and for 
       other purposes. <<NOTE: Dec. 20, 2020 -  [H.J. Res. 110]>> 

    Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United 
States of America in Congress assembled, <<NOTE: Extension of Continuing 
Appropriations Act, 2021.>>  That the Continuing Appropriations Act, 
2021 (division A of Public Law 116-159) <<NOTE: Ante, p. 711, 1041, 
1098.>>  is further amended by striking the date specified in section 
106(3) and inserting ``December 21, 2020''.

     This joint resolution may be cited as the ``Extension of Continuing 
Appropriations Act, 2021''.

    Approved December 20, 2020.
