Public Law 106-336
106th Congress

                                 An Act


 
To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 
  500 North Washington Street in Rockville, Maryland, as the ``Everett 
  Alvarez, Jr. Post Office Building''. <<NOTE: Oct. 19, 2000 -  [H.R. 
                                4484]>> 

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. DESIGNATION.

    The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 500 
North Washington Street in Rockville, Maryland, shall be known and 
designated as the ``Everett Alvarez, Jr. Post Office Building''.

SEC. 2. REFERENCES.

    Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other 
record of the United States to the facility referred to in section 1 
shall be deemed to be a reference to the ``Everett Alvarez, Jr. Post 
Office Building''.

    Approved October 19, 2000.
